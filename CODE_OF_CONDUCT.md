# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in SalienceGate free from harassment for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility and repairing harm;
- focusing on what is best for the community and project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, sexualized language or attention,
trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing private information without
permission, and conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `pieroc.96@gmail.com`. Reports are reviewed
promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality. The maintainer may remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy, and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project
in public spaces.
